# The Red Sea: full script

Israel leaves Egypt and is trapped between Pharaoh's army and the sea; God opens a way through. Exodus 13:17–15:21. It follows The Ten Plagues (the sea it pointed to) and leads toward Sinai. Working title: *The LORD Will Fight for You*.

**Runtime:** about 4 minutes (≈570 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Ten Commandments and Ten Plagues: the simple round PPL figures (straight stubby arms, dot eyes), SC scenery, FX fire, smoke and water. Reuses the Egypt, sea and people scenes from `videos/the-first-commandment/` and `videos/the-ten-plagues/`. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice, the lively take (`--ref-dir reference/own-voice-lively`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (Bible Gateway fill: `python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then `scripts/trim_verses.py`).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** At the Red Sea, Israel isn't brave and Moses isn't a magician. God fights for people who can do nothing but stand still, and then he tells them to walk.

**Misconception it overturns:** that this is the story of Moses parting the sea with a magic staff. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 ("The LORD will fight for you") and section 8 (Israel sings to the LORD, not to Moses).

**Spine:** God leads Israel the long way round, THEREFORE they end up camped by the sea. BUT Pharaoh changes his mind, THEREFORE the chariots come. THEREFORE the people panic. BUT Moses says, stand firm, the LORD will fight. THEREFORE the cloud moves between the armies and the wind blows all night. THEREFORE Israel walks through on dry ground. BUT Egypt follows, THEREFORE the sea comes back. THEREFORE Israel sings, and the story turns toward the wilderness and the mountain.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("God chose the long way"), 1:10 ("Then Pharaoh changed his mind"), 1:55 ("Moses answered with the strangest battle plan"), 2:55 ("And the people walked in"), 3:25 ("Then the morning came").

## 0. Cold open: trapped (0:00–0:25)

**Visual:** Night-blue sheet. A huge crowd of Israel on a narrow beach, the dark sea filling the top of the frame. Behind them, on the ridge, a long row of torches and the glint of chariot wheels and horses' heads. Children cling to parents; a mother pulls her child close. Cut to a tight shot of the waves, then a chariot wheel rolling over stones.
**On screen:** Black label: **TRAPPED** (one label at a time)
**Narration:**
> [grave] In front of them, the sea. [beat]
> [grave] Behind them, six hundred chariots. [grave] And nowhere left to run.
> [talk] Most people remember this story as Moses parting the sea with his staff, like a hero in a film.
> [pivot] But read it closely, and Moses isn't the hero. [beat]
> [talk] Neither is Israel.

**Transition:** Cut to a dawn-gold sheet.
