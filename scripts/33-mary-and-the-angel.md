# Mary and the Angel: full script

The angel Gabriel is sent to a young woman named Mary in the tiny village of Nazareth. She is told she will bear a son called Jesus, asks how this can be, and answers yes. Luke 1:26–38. Working title: *She Said Yes*. Third of the Christmas run (Advent 2026); stands alone.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈360 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Birth of Jesus: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Mary (the blue head-covering over a cream tunic), Joseph (brown striped robe, short beard), villagers. Backdrop kit: the hill village of Nazareth (a handful of flat-roofed houses, olive trees, a well), the inside of Mary's small house with a lamp and a water jar. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person. The angel Gabriel is light only: a tall column of white-gold light in the doorway with no face, figure or wings. Mary is never shown pregnant; the cost is shown only as neighbours whispering and Joseph turned away.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** God's biggest announcement went to a nobody in a nowhere town, and her yes cost her. She didn't know how it would work or what it would cost, and she said yes anyway.

**Misconception it overturns:** that Christmas began somewhere grand. It began in a village people joked about. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 1 (Nazareth) and section 6 (she said yes anyway).

**Spine:** Gabriel is sent to Nazareth, THEREFORE he greets Mary, BUT she is troubled, THEREFORE he tells her not to fear, AND that she will bear a son, BUT she asks how, THEREFORE he says nothing is impossible with God. Saying yes will cost her, BUT she says yes anyway.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("Can anything good"), 0:40 ("She was greatly troubled"), 1:10 ("How will this be"), 1:40 ("Saying yes would cost her"), 2:10 ("She said yes anyway").
