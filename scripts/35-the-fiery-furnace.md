# The Fiery Furnace: full script

King Nebuchadnezzar sets up a golden image and orders everyone to bow. Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego refuse, answer "but if not", are thrown into a furnace heated seven times hotter, and the king sees four men walking in the fire. Daniel 3. Working title: *But If Not*.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈370 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Daniel and the Lions: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego (young men in plain Judean robes: sand, sky blue, olive); Nebuchadnezzar (red and gold robe, a gold crown that sits down on his head with a band, black hair and beard); officials and musicians. Backdrop kit: the plain of Dura outside Babylon (ziggurat on the horizon), a towering golden image, the brick furnace with a wide mouth. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person. The fourth man in the fire is light only: a tall white-gold shape of light walking with them, no face. The soldiers killed by the heat (Daniel 3:22) are not shown or narrated. The fire is kit fire (FX), never hand-drawn.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Their faith didn't depend on being rescued. They said God is able to save us, but if not, we still won't bow. And they weren't alone in the fire.

**Misconception it overturns:** that the point is "God keeps you out of the fire." He didn't keep them out. He met them in it. Named in the cold open (four men), paid off in section 4 (the fourth man) and section 6 (not alone).

**Spine:** The king sets up a golden image, AND orders everyone to bow, BUT three men refuse, THEREFORE they are brought to the king, AND they say God is able, BUT if not, THEREFORE the furnace is heated seven times hotter, AND they are thrown in, BUT the king sees four men walking, THEREFORE he calls them out, AND not even their hair is singed.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("ninety feet of gold"), 0:40 ("Everyone bowed"), 1:00 ("But if not"), 1:30 ("seven times hotter"), 2:10 ("They didn't know").
