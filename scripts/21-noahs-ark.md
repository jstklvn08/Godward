# Noah's Ark: full script

God floods a violent world and saves one family and the animals in an ark, then promises never to do it again. Genesis 6–9. It follows Creation and the Fall (the world after Eden). Working title: *The Bow in the Cloud*.

**Runtime:** about 3½ minutes (≈500 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Jonah and Esther: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes, `kid:true` for the grandchildren-to-be is not needed: Noah's family is eight adults), SC animals in pairs (sheep, goats, oxen, donkeys, camels, lions, deer, birds, doves), the ark as a huge wooden box-ship, rain, a rainbow. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Noah's ark isn't a cute zoo story. It starts with God's heart grieving over a violent world, and it ends with a promise: God hangs up his bow and chooses mercy.

**Misconception it overturns:** that the flood story is mainly about cuddly animals two by two. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 1 (God grieves) and section 6 (the bow).

**Spine:** The world God made good is filled with violence, THEREFORE God is grieved and decides to start again. BUT Noah finds favour, THEREFORE God tells him to build an ark, AND Noah obeys. THEREFORE the animals and his family go in, AND God shuts the door. THEREFORE the flood comes, BUT God remembers Noah, THEREFORE the waters go down and the dove brings a leaf. THEREFORE God makes a covenant and sets his bow in the cloud.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("It starts with grief"), 0:55 ("But Noah"), 1:30 ("And the LORD shut him in"), 2:05 ("But God remembered Noah"), 2:40 ("Then God made a promise").
