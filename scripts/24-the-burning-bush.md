# The Burning Bush: full script

A shepherd in the desert meets God in a bush that burns but isn't burned up, and learns his name. Exodus 2:23–4:17. It comes between Joseph (how Israel came to Egypt) and the Ten Plagues. Working title: *I AM*.

**Runtime:** about 3¼ minutes (≈470 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Ten Plagues and Joseph: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes), Moses as the Ten Plagues' Moses but younger (grey-brown beard, shepherd's staff), sheep, the desert mountain of Horeb, the bush as a small thorny tree wrapped in living flame that never chars (God is never a figure: only fire, light and a voice drawn as rings). Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** At the burning bush, Moses keeps asking "Who am I?" God never really answers that. He answers a better question: who God is, and that he will be with him.

**Misconception it overturns:** that the story is about Moses being chosen because he was a great leader. He was an eighty-year-old shepherd, a runaway, full of excuses. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 ("I will be with you") and section 7.

**Spine:** Israel are slaves and cry out, AND God hears. Moses, a runaway shepherd, sees a bush on fire that doesn't burn up, THEREFORE he turns aside, AND God calls him. BUT Moses says "Who am I?", THEREFORE God says "I will be with you". BUT Moses asks God's name, THEREFORE God says "I AM WHO I AM". BUT Moses makes excuses, THEREFORE God gives him signs and his brother Aaron, AND Moses goes.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("Moses was eighty"), 0:55 ("Then he saw it"), 1:30 ("Who am I?"), 2:05 ("What is your name?"), 2:40 ("But Moses still had excuses").
