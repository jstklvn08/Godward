# Gideon: full script

God turns a frightened farmer into a leader, then shrinks his army from 32,000 to 300. Judges 6–8. It follows The Fall of Jericho (Israel in the land) a generation or two on, in the time of the judges. Working title: *Too Many*.

**Runtime:** about 3½ minutes (≈520 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Jericho, David and Goliath and Job: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes, `kid:true` for children), the backdrop kit (banded skies, hills, tents, caves, a winepress, foregrounds that belong to the place), SC animals (camels, a sheep fleece). Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** God didn't choose Gideon because he was strong. He made Gideon's army weaker on purpose, so that everyone would know who really saved them.

**Misconception it overturns:** that Gideon is a mighty warrior, and that the story is about being brave. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 ("too many") and section 7 ("The LORD will rule over you").

**Spine:** Midian raids Israel every harvest, THEREFORE Israel hides and cries out. AND God calls Gideon, BUT Gideon is the least in the weakest family, and afraid. THEREFORE God gives him signs, patiently. THEREFORE Gideon gathers 32,000 men, BUT God says they are too many. THEREFORE the army shrinks to 300, armed with trumpets, jars and torches. THEREFORE at night they blow, smash, shout, and the whole camp of Midian runs. BUT Gideon refuses to be king: the LORD will rule.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("Every harvest, they came"), 1:05 ("Gideon was hiding"), 1:50 ("Too many"), 2:35 ("In the middle of the night"), 3:10 ("They wanted to make Gideon king").

Below, the camp wakes in panic: tents shaking, camels running, the whole camp fleeing into the night (seen from far off, no fighting shown).
