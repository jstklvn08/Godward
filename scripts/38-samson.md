# Samson: full script

Samson is set apart to God before birth, no razor to touch his head. The Spirit of the LORD gives him astonishing strength, but he lives by whatever looks good to him. Delilah is paid to find his secret, his hair is cut, the LORD leaves him, and the Philistines capture and blind him. In their temple, with his hair growing back, he prays one last time and pushes the pillars. Judges 13–16. Working title: *Strong and Weak*.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈400 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Gideon and David: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Samson (big, scale about 1.2× the others, long dark hair in seven braids, a plain tan tunic); his parents; Delilah (deep teal robe, gold earrings); Philistine lords (bronze-and-red, feathered caps). Backdrop kit: the hills of Judah, a vineyard road, a Philistine city, Delilah's house at night, a prison mill, the temple of Dagon with two great pillars. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person; the angel is light only; the Spirit's strength is a rush of warm light. No blood or wounds. The lion fight is a cloud of dust with the lion running off. The blinding is never shown: in prison Samson wears a cloth band over his eyes. The collapse is pillars cracking and falling and a dust cloud; no bodies.

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Samson's strength was never in his hair. It was in God. His weakness was in his heart, and even at the end, God heard him when he asked to be remembered.

**Misconception it overturns:** that Samson is a superhero story. It's a story about a man who had everything and threw it away, and a God who still answered him. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 (the LORD had left him) and section 6 (strength was in God).

**Spine:** Samson is set apart before birth, THEREFORE no razor may touch his head. The Spirit gives him strength, BUT he lives by whatever looks good to him, THEREFORE he falls for Delilah, AND she is paid to find his secret, AND he tells her, THEREFORE his hair is cut, AND the LORD leaves him, THEREFORE he is captured and blinded. BUT his hair grows, AND he prays, THEREFORE God strengthens him one last time.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("No razor"), 0:40 ("the Spirit of the LORD rushed"), 1:00 ("Delilah"), 1:30 ("He did not know"), 2:00 ("please remember me").
