# Balaam's Donkey: full script

King Balak of Moab hires the prophet Balaam to curse Israel. God says no, but Balaam goes for the money. On the road his donkey sees the angel of the LORD and stops three times; Balaam beats her, the donkey speaks, then Balaam's eyes are opened. In the end, every time he opens his mouth to curse, only blessing comes out. Numbers 22–23. Working title: *The Donkey Saw First*.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈400 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Ruth and Abraham: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Balaam (a rich purple robe, a grey beard, a tall cap); his grey donkey (realistic size: her back at his chest, he rides her); King Balak of Moab (red robe, a crown that sits down on his head); Moabite officials with bags of silver. Backdrop kit: the red hills of Moab, a vineyard lane with stone walls on both sides, a high place with seven altars. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person. The angel of the LORD is light only: a tall column of white-gold light standing in the road, with a long blade of light for the drawn sword; no face, figure or wings. The beating is shown only as a raised stick and the donkey flinching; no blows land on screen.

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** The donkey could see what the prophet couldn't, because the prophet was looking at the money. And nobody can curse what God has blessed.

**Misconception it overturns:** that this is just a funny talking-animal story. It's about a famous prophet blinded by greed, and a God who turns curses into blessings. Named in the cold open (the donkey saw first), paid off in section 5 (Balaam's eyes opened) and section 7 (cannot revoke it).

**Spine:** Balak fears Israel, THEREFORE he hires Balaam to curse them, BUT God says they are blessed, BUT Balak offers more money, THEREFORE Balaam goes. The angel blocks the road, AND the donkey sees him, BUT Balaam doesn't, THEREFORE he beats her three times, THEREFORE the LORD opens her mouth, AND then Balaam's eyes. He goes to curse, BUT only blessing comes out.

**Re-hooks:** 0:12 ("Balak, king of Moab"), 0:40 ("they are blessed"), 1:00 ("the donkey saw"), 1:25 ("What have I done"), 2:00 ("I received a command to bless").
