# Simeon and Anna: full script

Forty days after the birth, Mary and Joseph bring the baby to the temple with the offering of the poor. An old man, Simeon, who was promised he would not die before he saw the Lord's Christ, takes the baby in his arms; then Anna, an eighty-four-year-old widow who never left the temple, gives thanks and tells everyone who was waiting. Luke 2:22–38. Working title: *Worth the Wait*. Fourth of the Christmas run (Advent 2026); stands alone.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈370 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Birth of Jesus: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Mary (blue head-covering over a cream tunic) and Joseph (brown striped robe) from the Birth of Jesus; Simeon (very old, white hair and beard, a plain grey robe, a stick); Anna (very old, a white head-covering over a deep plum robe). Backdrop kit: the temple courts in Jerusalem (tall stone colonnades, steps, morning light, crowds and sellers), a lamp-lit corner of the court at night. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person. The baby is only a small white swaddled bundle that glows softly, never a face. The Holy Spirit is shown only as a soft warm light falling on Simeon.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Most people in the temple walked straight past that baby. The two who knew him at once were the two who had spent their whole lives waiting.

**Misconception it overturns:** that waiting is wasted time. For Simeon and Anna, the waiting was what made them ready to see. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 (Anna) and section 5 (the ones who were waiting).

**Spine:** Simeon was promised he would see the Christ, BUT he grew old waiting. Mary and Joseph come to the temple, AND they can only afford two birds, BUT the Spirit leads Simeon there, THEREFORE he takes the baby in his arms, AND he is ready to die in peace. Then Anna, who never left the temple, sees him too, THEREFORE she tells everyone who was waiting.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("It had been revealed to him"), 0:40 ("Two birds"), 1:05 ("Lord, now you are letting"), 1:35 ("Then came Anna"), 2:10 ("Most people walked past").
