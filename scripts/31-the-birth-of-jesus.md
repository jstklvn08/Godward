# The Birth of Jesus: full script

Caesar orders a census, Joseph and Mary travel to Bethlehem, the baby is laid in a feeding trough, and an angel announces the birth to shepherds working the night shift. Luke 2:1–20. Working title: *No Innkeeper*. First of the Christmas run (Advent 2026); stands alone.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈390 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Prodigal Son: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Mary (a blue head-covering over a cream tunic), Joseph (a brown striped robe, short beard), shepherds (rough grey and brown wool, staffs, a boy shepherd with `kid:true`). Backdrop kit: the road to Bethlehem, Bethlehem's flat-roofed houses at night, the lower room of a crowded house where the animals are kept, the hillside fields at night with sheep and a small fire. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person. The baby is shown only as a small white swaddled bundle that glows softly, never with a face. The angel is light only: a tall shaft of white-gold light with no figure, face or wings. The heavenly host is a sky full of small points of light. No innkeeper figure anywhere except the crossed-out one in the cold open.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** God's king did not arrive in a palace. He was laid in a feeding trough, and the first people told were night-shift shepherds. The good news went first to the people nobody would have invited.

**Misconception it overturns:** the grumpy innkeeper of every Christmas play. Luke never mentions one. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 2 (no place, a manger) and section 6 (the invitation goes to the uninvited).

**Spine:** Caesar orders a census, THEREFORE Joseph and Mary travel to Bethlehem. The baby comes, BUT there is no place for them, THEREFORE he is laid in a manger. Shepherds are in the fields, AND an angel appears, AND the sky fills with angels, THEREFORE the shepherds run to Bethlehem, AND find him, AND tell everyone, BUT Mary keeps it all in her heart.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("In those days a decree"), 0:40 ("A manger is a feeding trough"), 1:05 ("Not priests. Not kings."), 1:35 ("Then the whole sky"), 2:10 ("God's king didn't arrive in a palace").
