# Abraham and Isaac: full script

God tests Abraham on Mount Moriah, stops him, and provides a ram. Genesis 12:1–3, 21:1–3, 22:1–14. It comes in the Genesis story between Noah and Joseph. Working title: *The LORD Will Provide*.

**Runtime:** about 3¼ minutes (≈460 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Joseph and Noah: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes, `kid:true` for Isaac as a boy), the backdrop kit (tents under stars, a donkey, a three-day road, a mountain, a stone altar, a ram in a thorn thicket), foregrounds that belong to the place. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint:** no knife is ever raised over Isaac. Isaac is shown carrying the wood and sitting on the altar stones; the knife stays in Abraham's belt. The angel of the LORD is only light and a voice (rings), never a figure.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Genesis 22 is one of the hardest stories in the Bible. But it doesn't end with a sacrifice. It ends with God stopping it, and providing the lamb himself.

**Misconception it overturns:** that the story shows a God who wants children sacrificed. Every other god of that land did; this God stops it, and provides. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 5 (the angel) and section 7 (Christians see the lamb).

**Spine:** God promises Abraham a family as many as the stars, BUT he and Sarah are old, THEREFORE Isaac is a miracle. BUT God tells Abraham to offer Isaac on a mountain, THEREFORE Abraham goes, AND says "God will provide." BUT at the last moment God stops him, THEREFORE a ram is caught in a thicket, AND Abraham names the place "The LORD will provide."

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("Abraham had waited"), 1:00 ("Then God tested Abraham"), 1:35 ("Where is the lamb?"), 2:10 ("Abraham, Abraham!"), 2:45 ("The LORD will provide").
