# The Prodigal Son: full script

Jesus's story of a younger son who wastes everything, comes home expecting to be a servant, and is met by a running father; and an older brother who won't come in. Luke 15:11–32. Working title: *While He Was Still a Long Way Off*.

**Runtime:** about 2¾ minutes (≈400 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Ruth and Joseph: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). The father is old (white beard, a deep blue robe); the younger son (`kid:false`, no beard, a bright yellow tunic that gets torn and grey); the older brother (a brown tunic, a beard, arms folded). Backdrop kit: a farm on green hills, a bright far-off city with music and coins, a famine-dry field with pigs, the long road home. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint:** the "wild living" is only coins flying away and a party's lights, never detail. The pigs are friendly cartoon pigs.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** The story is called the prodigal son, but its hero is the father. He runs. And the story ends with a question for the older brother, the one who never left, which Jesus leaves open for us.

**Misconception it overturns:** that it's only a story about a bad son who said sorry. Jesus told it to religious people who were grumbling that he ate with sinners (Luke 15:2). Named in the cold open, paid off in section 5 (the father runs) and section 6 (the older brother).

**Spine:** A son asks for his inheritance early, THEREFORE he leaves AND wastes it all. BUT a famine comes, THEREFORE he feeds pigs, AND comes to his senses. He heads home to ask to be a servant, BUT his father sees him far off AND runs, THEREFORE there's a feast. BUT the older brother is angry AND won't come in, THEREFORE the father goes out to him too.

**Re-hooks:** 0:20 ("A man had two sons"), 0:50 ("Then a famine came"), 1:20 ("But while he was still a long way off"), 1:55 ("But the older brother"), 2:25 ("Jesus never tells us").
