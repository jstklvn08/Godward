# Joseph: full script

A favourite son is sold by his brothers, rises to rule Egypt, and forgives them. Genesis 37, 39–45, 50. It follows Noah in the Genesis story and leads toward the Ten Plagues (how Israel came to Egypt). Working title: *God Meant It for Good*.

**Runtime:** about 3¾ minutes (≈540 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Ten Plagues, Elijah and Noah: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes, `kid:true` for young Joseph and Benjamin), the backdrop kit (Canaan hills with sheep and tents, a dry pit, a camel caravan, Egyptian palace and pyramids, a prison, grain stores), foregrounds that belong to the place. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Joseph's story isn't really about a colourful coat or dreams. It's about a God who quietly turns other people's evil into rescue, and a brother who chooses to forgive.

**Misconception it overturns:** that the story's point is the "technicolour coat" or "follow your dreams". Named in the cold open, paid off in section 6 (the reveal) and section 7 (Genesis 50:20).

**Spine:** Jacob loves Joseph most, THEREFORE his brothers hate him, AND sell him as a slave. BUT the LORD is with Joseph, even in prison. THEREFORE when Pharaoh dreams, Joseph explains it, AND is put in charge of Egypt. THEREFORE when famine comes, his brothers come to buy food and bow before him, not knowing him. BUT Joseph tests them, AND Judah offers his own life for Benjamin, THEREFORE Joseph reveals himself and forgives them.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("Joseph was his father's favourite"), 1:00 ("So they sold him"), 1:35 ("But the LORD was with Joseph"), 2:15 ("And then the famine came"), 2:55 ("Joseph couldn't hold it in").
