# The Wise Men: full script

Wise men from the east follow a star to Jerusalem, King Herod is troubled, the star leads them to the child in Bethlehem, they worship and give gifts, and they go home by another way. Matthew 2:1–12. Working title: *Not Three Kings*. Second of the Christmas run (Advent 2026); stands alone.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈380 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Birth of Jesus: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). The wise men: three robed travellers in deep jewel colours (indigo, plum, teal) with tall soft hats, not crowns, plus their servants and camels. Herod: an old king with a gold crown and a red robe, stiff pose. Mary (the blue head-covering from the Birth of Jesus). Backdrop kit: a desert night under a huge sky, Jerusalem's walls and Herod's palace, the road to Bethlehem, a small house in Bethlehem. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person. The child Jesus is never shown with a face: only a small, soft glow beside Mary. The dream is a ring of light over a sleeping man, no figure. Herod's later massacre is not shown or narrated.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** One story, two kings. Herod had a throne and was terrified of losing it. The wise men had treasure and travelled for months to give it away. Same child, two opposite responses.

**Misconception it overturns:** three kings kneeling at the manger. Matthew never says there were three, never calls them kings, and they found the child in a house. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 (the house) and section 6 (two kings).

**Spine:** Wise men see a star, THEREFORE they travel to Jerusalem, AND ask where the king is, BUT Herod is the king, THEREFORE he is troubled, AND sends them to Bethlehem with a lie. The star goes before them, THEREFORE they find the child, AND worship, AND give gifts, BUT they are warned in a dream, THEREFORE they go home by another way.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("Wise men from the east"), 0:45 ("There was already a king"), 1:20 ("And behold, the star"), 1:45 ("going into the house"), 2:15 ("Same child").
