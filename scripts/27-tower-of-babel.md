# The Tower of Babel: full script

After the flood, people build a city and a tower to reach the heavens, and God scatters them by mixing their languages. Genesis 11:1–9, with Acts 2:1–11 at the end. It comes in the Genesis story between Noah and Abraham. Working title: *Make a Name*.

**Runtime:** about 2¾ minutes (≈400 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Noah and Abraham: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes), the backdrop kit (the flat plain of Shinar with a river, brick kilns, a ziggurat-like stepped tower that grows sheet by sheet, scaffolds and ladders). Speech is drawn as little torn-paper speech bubbles: the same simple symbols for everyone at first, then different scripts (shapes, not real words) once the languages are mixed. God is never a figure: only light coming down from above. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Babel isn't a story about God being afraid of tall buildings. It's about people trying to reach heaven and make a name for themselves, and a God who comes down. At Pentecost, he does it again, the other way round.

**Misconception it overturns:** that God was threatened by the tower. The story's joke is that the tower everyone thought reached heaven was so small God had to "come down" to see it. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 3 (God came down) and section 6 (Pentecost).

**Spine:** Everyone speaks one language, THEREFORE they build a city and a tower to reach the heavens, AND make a name for themselves. BUT God comes down to see it, THEREFORE he mixes their language, AND they can't understand each other, THEREFORE they stop building and scatter. BUT at Pentecost, God comes down again, AND people from every language hear the same good news, THEREFORE what was scattered begins to be gathered.

**Re-hooks:** 0:20 ("After the flood"), 0:50 ("Let us make a name"), 1:20 ("But the LORD came down"), 1:50 ("So they stopped building"), 2:15 ("Centuries later").

Families pack up and walk away in different directions, each group with its own bubble shape. The half-finished tower stands alone.
