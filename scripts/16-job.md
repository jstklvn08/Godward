# Job: full script

A good man loses everything in one day, and asks God why. Job 1–2, 13, 38–42. A stand-alone story from the Bible's wisdom books. Working title: *Where Were You?*

**Runtime:** about 4 minutes (≈600 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as David and Goliath and Jericho: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes, `kid:true` for children), the backdrop kit (banded skies, hills, tents, a big stone house, foregrounds that belong to the place), SC animals (oxen, donkeys, sheep, camels, then the wild animals of Job 38–39). Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** The book of Job never tells Job why he suffered. It gives him something better than an answer: God himself.

**Misconception it overturns:** that suffering always means you did something wrong (Job's friends' answer), and that the lesson is simply "be patient like Job". Named in the cold open, paid off in section 5 (the friends) and section 8 (God says Job's friends were wrong).

**Spine:** Job is good and blessed, BUT the accuser says he only loves God for what he gets, THEREFORE Job loses everything in one day. BUT Job still blesses God's name. THEREFORE his friends come, and sit with him, BUT then they tell him it must be his fault. THEREFORE Job cries out for God to answer. AND God answers, out of a storm, BUT not with a reason: with himself. THEREFORE Job says, now I see you, and God restores him.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("Job had everything"), 1:00 ("Then, in one day"), 1:50 ("Then his friends came"), 2:30 ("And then God answered"), 3:35 ("He never found out why").
