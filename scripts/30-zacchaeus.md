# Zacchaeus: full script

A rich, hated tax collector climbs a tree to see Jesus, and Jesus invites himself to his house. Luke 19:1–10. Working title: *Come Down*.

**Runtime:** about 2¼ minutes (≈340 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as the Prodigal Son: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Zacchaeus is short (`kid:true` proportions but with a beard and a rich purple robe with gold trim, a money bag). The crowd in many colours. Jesus is not shown as a figure: a warm glow in the middle of the crowd, the crowd parting around it, and his words on torn strips. Backdrop kit: Jericho's palm-lined street, a sycamore-fig tree, a rich house with a table. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Zacchaeus went looking for Jesus. But the story ends by saying Jesus came looking for him. Grace came first; the change came after.

**Misconception it overturns:** that Jesus came to his house because Zacchaeus changed. He changed because Jesus came. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 (the crowd grumbles, then Zacchaeus stands up) and section 5 (Luke 19:10).

**Spine:** Zacchaeus is rich AND a chief tax collector, THEREFORE everyone hates him. He wants to see Jesus BUT he's too short, THEREFORE he climbs a tree. Jesus stops AND calls him by name, AND invites himself to his house, BUT the crowd grumbles. THEREFORE Zacchaeus gives half to the poor AND pays back four times, AND Jesus says salvation has come.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("Zacchaeus was rich"), 0:45 ("So he ran ahead"), 1:10 ("Zacchaeus, hurry"), 1:35 ("But everyone grumbled"), 2:00 ("For the Son of Man").
