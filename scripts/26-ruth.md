# Ruth: full script

A foreign widow refuses to leave her mother-in-law, gleans in a stranger's field, and ends up in the family line of King David. Ruth 1–4. It sits in the time of the judges, between Gideon and David and Goliath. Working title: *Where You Go, I Will Go*.

**Runtime:** about 3 minutes (≈430 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Joseph and Abraham: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes), the backdrop kit (Bethlehem's barley fields on gold hills, a dusty road, Moab's red hills, a threshing floor at night, the town gate). Naomi is old (white headscarf, grey robe), Ruth is young (a deep red scarf so she reads as a foreigner), Boaz is middle-aged (brown beard, green robe). Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint:** the threshing-floor night is shown simply: Boaz asleep by the grain heap, Ruth sitting quietly at his feet, wrapped in her shawl; then Boaz awake and kind. Nothing more.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Ruth isn't a romance first. It's a story about loyal love (the Hebrew word *hesed*): a foreigner who chose to belong to God's people, and a God who was quietly at work in the ordinary days of an empty, bitter family.

**Misconception it overturns:** that God only works through miracles and big moments. There isn't a single miracle in Ruth. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 5 (she "happened" to come to Boaz's field) and section 7 (David).

**Spine:** Famine sends Naomi's family to Moab, BUT her husband and both sons die, THEREFORE she heads home empty. Ruth refuses to leave her, AND goes with her to Bethlehem. BUT they're poor, THEREFORE Ruth gleans in the fields, AND happens to come to Boaz's field. Boaz is kind, THEREFORE Naomi sends Ruth to ask him to redeem the family, AND Boaz does it properly at the gate. THEREFORE Ruth marries Boaz, AND their son is the grandfather of King David.

**Re-hooks:** 0:25 ("Naomi had lost everything"), 0:55 ("Where you go, I will go"), 1:30 ("She happened to come"), 2:05 ("Naomi had a plan"), 2:35 ("Their son was called Obed").
