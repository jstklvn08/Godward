# Lazarus: full script

Jesus hears that his friend Lazarus is ill, and waits two more days. By the time he reaches Bethany, Lazarus has been in the tomb four days. Martha meets him on the road, Mary weeps at his feet, Jesus weeps, and then he calls Lazarus out of the tomb. John 11:1–44. Working title: *Late Is Not Never*.

**Runtime:** about 2½ minutes (≈380 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Zacchaeus: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). Martha (a russet robe, practical, a head-covering), Mary (sage green, a white head-covering), Lazarus (a plain cream tunic; in the tomb scene wrapped head to foot in white linen strips with only the face open), mourners in grey, the disciples. Backdrop kit: a dusty road into the hill village of Bethany, olive trees, a rock-cut tomb in a hillside with a big round stone. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person, and Jesus is shown only as a warm glow (a column of warm light walking with the disciples), never a face or figure. When "Jesus wept", the glow dims and softly shimmers; no face. No dead body is shown; the tomb is closed until Lazarus walks out alive.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** Jesus came late on purpose, wept anyway, and then called a dead man by name. Late is not the same as never.

**Misconception it overturns:** that if God loved you, he'd come straight away. John says Jesus loved them, so he stayed two more days. Named in the cold open, paid off in section 4 (Jesus wept) and section 6 (late is not never).

**Spine:** Lazarus is ill, AND his sisters send for Jesus, BUT Jesus waits two days, THEREFORE Lazarus dies. Jesus arrives four days late, AND Martha says if you had been here, BUT Jesus says I am the resurrection, THEREFORE she believes. Mary weeps, AND Jesus weeps, BUT then he tells them to roll the stone away, AND calls Lazarus out, THEREFORE the dead man walks out.

**Re-hooks:** 0:15 ("he whom you love"), 0:45 ("four days"), 1:10 ("I am the resurrection"), 1:35 ("Jesus wept"), 2:00 ("Lazarus, come out").
