# Jesus Calms the Storm: full script

Jesus and his disciples cross the Sea of Galilee at night. A great windstorm swamps the boat while Jesus sleeps; the disciples wake him, he rebukes the wind, and there is a great calm. The disciples end more afraid than they began. Mark 4:35–41. Working title: *Who Then Is This?*

**Runtime:** about 2 minutes (≈320 words with the scripture at 140–150 words a minute, plus pauses).
**Look:** glowing-collage style, the same kit as Jonah: the simple round PPL figures (one straight stubby arm each, dot eyes). The disciples: fishermen in rough tunics (Peter in a brown tunic with a grey beard, Andrew, James, John younger), wet hair in the storm. Backdrop kit: the Sea of Galilee at evening between hills, the open sea at night in a storm (lightning, rain, waves over the bow), the sea afterwards flat as glass under the stars. Vertical 9:16 only.
**Restraint and doctrine:** God is never shown as a person, and Jesus is shown only as a warm glow (asleep: a soft glow on a cushion in the stern; standing: a column of warm light), never a face or figure. No one drowns; the danger is waves and water in the boat.
**Voice:** the narrator's own voice (`voice/reference/own-voice/`), natural edit.
**Translation:** ESV (`python scripts/fill_esv.py --bg`, then trim by hand to the "keep only" text).

## Thesis and spine

**Thesis:** The story doesn't end with "the storm stopped". It ends with a question: "Who then is this?" The disciples were afraid of the storm, then more afraid of the one who stopped it.

**Misconception it overturns:** that this is a story about God fixing your problem. It's a story about finding out who is in the boat with you. Named in the cold open (he was asleep), paid off in section 5 (great fear) and section 6 (the right question).

**Spine:** Jesus says let us go across, THEREFORE they set out, BUT a great windstorm arises, AND the boat fills, BUT Jesus is asleep, THEREFORE they wake him, AND he rebukes the wind, THEREFORE there is a great calm, BUT now they are more afraid, THEREFORE they ask who he is.

**Re-hooks:** 0:12 ("Let us go across"), 0:35 ("a great windstorm"), 0:55 ("Teacher, do you not care"), 1:15 ("Peace! Be still!"), 1:40 ("Who then is this").
