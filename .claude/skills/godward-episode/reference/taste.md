# The user's taste rules (from episode 1 feedback)

Every rule here came from the user's own feedback while making "Creation and the Fall". Check a
draft against this list before sending it; it is cheaper than another round of notes.

## Format
- Primary deliverable: vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. The user writes it
  as "9:6". It must be remade to fit the tall frame, never cropped from landscape.
- Vertical is the only format (user, Nov 2026: "change skill to vertical 9:6"). No landscape cut
  unless the user asks for one.
- Instagram feed thumbnail: 4:5 (1080×1350), key content inside the centre 1012 px (grid crop).
- Send short samples early and often ("give me a sample when possible"). Never surprise the user
  with a 45-minute full render they haven't previewed.

## Picture
- Never leave empty bands at the top or bottom in vertical. Fill with paper, sky, ground or a
  foreground layer, in every scene.
- Make scenes immersive: "dive deeper ... like going thru a painting". Fly into a panel full-screen
  with a header, rather than showing a grid of small panels.
- Not stagnant: something always moves. Characters breathe, shift weight and gesture while their
  line is spoken; verses appear and leave about a second after they are narrated.
- Camera shake and punch-in only on 2-3 big focus beats per episode; steady otherwise.
- Calm camera ("in the first few sec of the vid, there is too much motion, zoom in and out"): at most one slow
  push-in or pan per shot (2-3 s or longer), or a still hold. No zooming in and back out; cut to change framing.
  Visual pace comes from cuts, new scenes and characters acting, not from the camera.
- Frame the subject of the line ("this scene should focus on the man"). Don't go too tight
  ("too zoomed in ... see the whole picture"): use a wide shot when the whole scene matters.
- Scale and motion must make sense: the serpent was too big and moved back and forth; it should
  crawl forward once and stop.
- Animals should look like distinct, recognisable animals, not blobs.
- Rich detail where the story is big (creation days, the heavens): darkness and heaven were drawn
  out in the opening, not skipped.
- Text must never be hidden, cut off or covered (a yellow label at 242 s was invisible; a "GENESIS
  1–3" label covered a word; a tag sat on the serpent).

## People and settings (Ten Commandments and Ten Plagues feedback, Nov 2026)
- People are the simple round figures (PPL): big round head, dot eyes, small smile, rounded tunic, stub legs, and each
  arm ONE short straight stub with a round hand; no elbow ("u dont have to put extra joints unless nessesarry").
  Resting arms hang straight. Children get `kid:true` (no beard).
- No hand or arm ever crosses a face, the person's own or anyone else's (a raised arm goes up and out; a hug or
  carry arm goes behind the other head; a hand on a shoulder rests below the chin).
- Houses must not be plain boxes ("houses look bland, refine it"): mud-brick courses, flat roofs with parapets and
  beam ends, upper rooms, ladders, awnings, lattice windows, lamplight, jars, laundry, smoke.
- Backgrounds must not be bland ("background looks bland fix it"): no big empty gradient skies, no repeated
  grass-and-rock foreground, no small subject in a thin band. Every scene gets a sky in its own mood colour,
  landmark silhouettes, a real mid-ground and a foreground that belongs to the place, plus matching particles.

## Captions
- Below the picture (vertical: about 68% of the height, y≈1300), clear of the platform's buttons.
- One to three words at a time ("too much words at once ... bores ppl"), the spoken word in yellow.
- Multi-line captions need clear space between lines; split long lines in two.

## Sound
- Narration always clearly on top. The music bed still audible (about 30 dB under the voice), never
  gone.
- Sounds match the picture: water for the sea, animal calls for animals, a flame roar for the sun,
  birdsong in the garden, wind and rumble in darkness.
- An angelic choir and bells to open, loud ("make the angelic ambient louder", peak at 3 s).
- Cut the music just before a "God spoke" moment and hit a loud thunder.
- No rattly or squeaky effects (marker squeaks became soft swishes); chirps quiet.
- God has no footsteps; use a warm wind.

## Voice
- The narrator's own voice, natural edit: slower, real pauses, varied spacing ("more human").
- Every word must match the script; listen-check the very short lines.

## Content
- Scripture exact, always ESV (via the API; the user chose ESV for the channel). KJV only if the
  user asks. Paraphrase never shown as scripture.
- God never depicted as a person: light, wind, fire, words.
- Order of creation respected (light before the sun; the sun on Day 4).
- People modestly clothed; cherubim as wings of light, not figures.
- Interpretations attributed ("Christians have long read this as...").
- No brand names, logos or recognisable characters from other channels; no copying another
  channel's mascot. The look is our own "glowing collage", inspired by explainer channels.
