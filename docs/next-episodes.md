**Length has drifted down.** The first 20 episodes run 3:06–4:30. The newer ones run 1:35–2:59. That matters for YouTube:
- **YouTube Shorts:** the cap has been 3 minutes since October 2024 ([Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/youtube-shorts-is-making-a-huge-change-to-its-maximum-video-length-and-it-will-affect-creators), [Descript](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be)). Episodes 1–20 (3:06 and longer, outro included) will post as ordinary YouTube videos, not Shorts.
- **Instagram Reels:** Buffer's study of 2.7 million Reels found that 90–179-second Reels got 7.4% more reach than an account's baseline, and Reels of 180 seconds or more did worse. Only Reels of 3 minutes or less are shown to non-followers ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length)).
- **New episodes:** aim for **2:00–2:50 including the 6.5 s outro**. That's about 250–340 narrated words, well under the 580–640 words in the skill's kick-off note, which dates from the 4-minute episodes.

## The arc so far, and the gaps

The catalogue now covers most famous Old Testament stories, and the Christmas set covers the main Nativity scenes. Each video stands alone, so a gap matters for search and seasonal timing more than for running order.

- **Christmas gaps:** Joseph's side of the story (Matthew 1:18–25), Zechariah, Elizabeth and the birth of John the Baptist (Luke 1:5–25, 57–80), Mary visiting Elizabeth (Luke 1:39–56), and the flight to Egypt (Matthew 2:13–23).
- **Exodus gaps:** Moses in the basket (Exodus 2), the manna (Exodus 16), water from the rock (Exodus 17), and above all the golden calf (Exodus 32), which happens right after the commandments.
- **The content report's ranked list:** of Jericho, Job, David and Goliath, Samson, Daniel and Revelation, Samson and Revelation are the two not made yet.
- **Other well-known stories not made yet:** the fiery furnace (Daniel 3), the writing on the wall (Daniel 5), Cain and Abel (Genesis 4), Jacob (Genesis 27–32), Solomon's wisdom (1 Kings 3), Jesus' miracles (walking on water, feeding the five thousand, Lazarus), and the Easter story. Easter 2027 is 28 March, so that one belongs in the February planning.

## What the research says

**Old Testament stories pull the most views.** The repo's own `Godward-Content-Report.html` (9 Nov 2026) found:
- US Bible-story channels average about 268K views per video, against 80K for Bible study and 61K for prayer.
- BibleProject's Old Testament overviews get about 30% more views per video than its New Testament ones.
- Matthew is the only Gospel in the top 12 books. The report also warns against two-part videos.

**On TikTok, famous scenes travel.** Crosswalk reported that one creator's AI-made Bible clips drew about 6.8M views for Daniel in the lions' den, and nearly 20M in total for Judas, Noah, Eve, Jonah, Mary and David ([Crosswalk](https://www.crosswalk.com/headlines/contributors/michael-foust/christians-split-over-viral-ai-videos-that-put-bible-characters-on-tiktok.html); [Christian Post](https://www.christianpost.com/news/ai-bible-shorts-envision-life-with-iphone.html)). Those are AI-video accounts, and the figures are as reported, not checked. They do show which characters people already know and click.

**Seasonal timing:**
- **Searches:** Pew's Google Trends analysis found "church" searches peak at Easter, with Christmas usually second ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2014/04/18/when-easter-and-christmas-near-more-americans-search-online-for-church/)).
- **YouTube:** interest in Christmas content builds from mid-December and peaks in the third week (14–20 Dec this year) ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/top-10-christmas-keywords-youtube/)).
- **Faith channels:** they launch their Christmas films around the start of Advent. In 2025, the Latter-day Saints' Luke 2 video "Light the World" came out on 30 November ([Church News](https://www.thechurchnews.com/living-faith/2025/11/30/light-the-world-video-and-initiative-add-christ-light-to-traditions/)). K-LOVE released a Mary's-eye Nativity film and BibleProject an Advent visual podcast ([K-LOVE](https://www.klove.com/faith/news/god-stories/virgin-birth-new-k-love-on-demand-christmas-film-brings-mary-s-story-to-life-andpodcast-57156), [BibleProject](https://bibleproject.com/videos/collections/advent-visual-podcast/)).
- **The upshot:** a Christmas episode is worth most if it's posted between early December and Christmas Day. Godward's existing four cover the usual scenes, so a new one should show something the big Nativity films skip.

**How firm this is.** I found no 2026 ranking of Bible-story Shorts by views, and no platform data on Nativity searches. Most "Bible shorts" guides online come from AI-video tool vendors selling a product. The seasonal points come from vidIQ and Pew, and the story-popularity points from the content report. Treat the ranking below as judgement on that evidence.

## Top 5

### 1. Joseph's Dream: Matthew 1:18–25
- **Hook:** "He found out she was pregnant, and he knew the child wasn't his."
- **Why now:** it's the one big Christmas scene the set is missing, told from a man's side, which Nativity films rarely centre. Ready by about 10 December, it lands before the vidIQ peak and fills the gap in an Advent run (see below).
- **Fit:** it reuses the Nazareth, Mary and night-sky scenes from Mary and the Angel and the Birth of Jesus, so it's the quickest episode to make. Genesis Joseph was also a dreamer, which gives the cold open a quiet echo ("Another Joseph. Another dream.").
- **Runtime:** about 1:45–2:00 with the outro (about 220–260 words). The passage is only eight verses.
- **Title note:** "Joseph" is already taken by the Genesis episode, so use "Joseph's Dream" or "The Man Who Stayed".

### 2. The Golden Calf: Exodus 32:1–35 (optionally 34:6–7 to close)
- **Hook:** "Forty days after they heard God speak, they melted their earrings into a calf."
- **Why now:** it's the biggest evergreen gap in the Exodus run and the obvious "what happened next" after ten commandment episodes. Make it in January, when the Christmas window has closed.
- **Fit:** it reuses the Sinai, tablets and camp scenes from the commandment episodes and the Red Sea crowd. FX fire handles the calf being melted, then burned.
- **Runtime:** about 2:30–2:50 (about 300–330 words). Keep it to verses 1–6, 15–20 and 30–35, so it stays under 3 minutes.

### 3. Samson: Judges 13–16 (centred on 16:4–30)
- **Hook:** "The strongest man in the Bible lost everything to a haircut."
- **Why now:** it's the only story left from the content report's ranked list that's still a story. Judges ranked 6th by overview views (5.7M).
- **Fit:** it's an action story with a tragic hero, the same register as Gideon (also from Judges). The temple-pillars finale is a strong last image.
- **Runtime:** about 2:40–2:50. Four chapters is a lot for 3 minutes, so open on Delilah and tell the birth in two lines.

### 4. The Fiery Furnace: Daniel 3:1–30
- **Hook:** "Three men wouldn't bow. So the king made the fire seven times hotter."
- **Why now:** Daniel is one of the most-clicked characters on TikTok (the 6.8M figure above), and this is his book's other famous scene. It gives Daniel and the Lions a companion.
- **Fit:** the FX fire kit was made for this. Babylon scenery carries over from Daniel and the Lions.
- **Runtime:** about 2:15–2:30.

### 5. The Flight to Egypt: Matthew 2:13–23
- **Hook:** "Jesus' first journey was as a refugee, running from a king."
- **Why now:** it runs straight on from the Wise Men and suits the week after Christmas (28 December is the traditional day for Herod's victims). It also links the Christmas set to the Exodus run, since Matthew 2:15 quotes Hosea 11:1, "out of Egypt I called my son."
- **Fit:** it reuses the Egypt backdrop kit from the Ten Plagues and the Red Sea, and the Wise Men's Herod.
- **Runtime:** about 1:50–2:10. Tell verse 16 gently: soldiers' silhouettes and a weeping mother, nothing graphic.

**Honourable mentions:**
- Zechariah and Elizabeth (Luke 1:5–25, 57–80): a second Advent option if there's time.
- The Manna (Exodus 16).
- Moses in the Basket (Exodus 2).
- The Writing on the Wall (Daniel 5).
- Jesus Walks on Water (Matthew 14:22–33).
- Plan the Easter story for March 2027.

**Make Joseph's Dream (Matthew 1:18–25) next.** It's the only pick whose value depends on timing, and the time is now. It's short and reuses scenes already built, so it can be ready before mid-December, and it completes the Christmas set the channel already has instead of competing with it.
