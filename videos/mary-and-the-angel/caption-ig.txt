God's biggest announcement didn't go to a palace or a temple. It went to a young woman in a village people joked about. "Can anything good come out of Nazareth?"

An angel told Mary she would bear a son and call his name Jesus. She asked the obvious question, and heard: "For nothing will be impossible with God."

📖 Luke 1:26–38 (ESV)

Saying yes would cost her. She didn't know how it would work, or what it would cost. She said yes anyway.
