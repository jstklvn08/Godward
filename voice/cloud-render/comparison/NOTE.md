# Voice setting recommendation

Recommended setting for the teen and adult Bible story clips: **exaggeration 0.3, CFG 0.5** (`b-restrained-ex0.3-cfg0.5.wav`). The Chatterbox README says the default of 0.5/0.5 suits most prompts, that a lower CFG of about 0.3 slows fast delivery, and that higher exaggeration makes speech faster and more dramatic. That matches the measurements of the clip 3 line here (one seed, seed 1, CPU). At 0.7/0.3 (d), speech ends at 5.9 s, about 4.2 words a second, which is rushed for scripture. At 0.5/0.5 (a), it ends at 7.2 s (3.5 words a second). At 0.3/0.5 (b), it ends at 7.7 s (3.2 words a second), with the longest pauses at the phrase breaks (0.20-0.44 s) and the widest pitch spread of the four (5.6 semitones, against 4.9-5.1 for the others). At 0.4/0.3 (c), it is slowest, ending at 8.3 s (3.0 words a second), with 5.0 semitones. So b is the most unhurried while still leaving about 2.3 s of the 10 s clip. If a calmer read is wanted where the clip has room, c is the fallback. Pitch spread is only a rough proxy for expressiveness, and these numbers come from one seed per setting. **Nobody has listened to this audio.** The recommendation rests on the README and on measurements only, including an automatic transcript check that found no dropped words. Someone needs to listen before anything is chosen or published.
